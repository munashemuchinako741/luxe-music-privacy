Privacy Policy

Luxe Music is an offline-first local audio playback and media management application. Your library stays yours.

1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Offline-First

Luxe Music operates primarily on your device. We believe your personal music library, listening habits, playlists, and settings belong exclusively to you.

  • No remote tracking: We do not track, profile, sell, or rent your personal data.
  • No third-party ads: The App does not contain third-party advertising frameworks or ad-tracking networks.
  • Local storage: All app data, including playlists, playback queues, listening history, equalizer settings, and app configurations, is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Access and Process

Luxe Music accesses only the data required to deliver core music playback and app customization features.

Local Audio Files & Metadata

Data accessed: Audio files (MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC, WAV, and similar formats) stored on your device or SD card, alongside metadata such as title, artist, album, genre, duration, track number, and artwork.

Purpose: To discover, organize, display, and play your audio files.

Storage & transmission: Metadata is indexed and cached locally. Your audio files and metadata are never uploaded to external servers.

Speech and Voice Commands (Optional)

Data accessed: Audio input recorded through your microphone when you explicitly activate voice search or voice controls.

Purpose: To convert spoken commands into app navigation and playback actions.

Storage & transmission: Speech processing is handled locally or through your operating system's native speech recognition service. Luxe Music never stores, saves, or transmits audio recordings to external servers.

Motion & Device Sensors (Optional)

Accelerometer and motion sensor events may be processed in real time to enable controls such as shake to skip or play/pause. Motion data is never logged or transmitted.

Biometric & Security Data (Optional)

Luxe Music may use your device's fingerprint, Face ID, or system passcode authentication when App Lock is enabled. The App relies on Android BiometricPrompt or iOS LocalAuthentication and never receives, sees, or stores raw biometric data.

Device State & Network Connectivity

Basic system status, including network availability, screen state, and audio output routing, may be used to pause playback when headphones disconnect, handle audio focus during calls or notifications, and provide online status checks when you open external links.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Luxe Music does not integrate third-party data analytics services such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or Mixpanel, and does not run third-party advertising services.

If you click an external link within the App, your default browser will open and the destination website's privacy policy will govern your interaction on that site.

5. Data Security and Retention

  • Local encryption: Sensitive local credentials, such as an optional app passcode, are encrypted using standard platform secure storage mechanisms through Android KeyStore or iOS Keychain.
  • Data retention and removal: All app data resides strictly on your device. You can clear cached data, settings, and playlists by clearing App Data in your device settings or uninstalling the application. Uninstalling Luxe Music does not delete physical music files.

6. Children's Privacy

Luxe Music is a general-audience audio player and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or 16 in the European Union). Because Luxe Music does not collect personal information online, it complies with applicable children's privacy requirements, including COPPA and GDPR.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your region, including under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights regarding personal data. Because Luxe Music stores data locally and does not collect or transmit personal data to remote servers:

  • Access and export: Your playlists, favorites, and history are accessible inside the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling Luxe Music.
  • Permission control: You can manage or revoke hardware permissions in your operating system settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates, platform policy revisions, or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published within the application or repository with an updated “Last Updated” date.
